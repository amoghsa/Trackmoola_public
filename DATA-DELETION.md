# Data Deletion — TrackMoola

**Effective date:** June 4, 2026
**Last updated:** September 30, 2026

This page explains how to delete your data from TrackMoola and, where applicable, from third-party services the App interacts with.

If you have any questions, contact us at **support@amoghsa.com**.

---

## 1. Your financial data is on your device

TrackMoola is an **offline-first** app. The financial information you enter — income, savings, debts, expenses, properties, dependents, and calculation results — is stored **locally on your device only**. We have no backend server; we cannot access or hold this data.

**To delete all on-device data permanently:**

- **iOS:** Go to your device **Settings → General → iPhone Storage → TrackMoola → Delete App**. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data immediately.
- **Android:** Go to **Settings → Apps → TrackMoola → Storage → Clear Data**, then uninstall the app. Clear Data wipes the local database; uninstalling removes the app entirely.

Either action is instant and irreversible. No request to us is needed.

---

## 2. Optional cloud sync (your own iCloud or Google Drive)

If you enabled cloud sync (a Pro-tier feature), a copy of your data is stored in **your own cloud account** — not ours. We never hold this data.

**To delete synced data:**

- **iCloud (iOS):** Open **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → TrackMoola** and choose "Delete Data". Alternatively, visit [iCloud.com](https://www.icloud.com) and manage app data from there. You can also disable iCloud sync for TrackMoola at **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Apps Using iCloud → TrackMoola (toggle off)** before deleting the app.
- **Google Drive (Android):** Your synced data lives in a private app folder. Go to [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com) → **Settings → Manage Apps → TrackMoola → Delete hidden app data**. This removes the app-private folder and all its contents.

Deleting the app without first clearing cloud sync does not automatically remove the cloud copy. Follow the steps above if you want the cloud copy removed.

---

## 3. Partner-shared data (Couples tier)

If you used the Couples / Partner Sync feature, a copy of your shared household data may exist in your **partner's** iCloud or Google Drive account as well as your own. Deleting your own cloud copy does not remove your partner's copy.

To remove shared data from your partner's account, both of you should follow the cloud deletion steps in Section 2. If you need help co-ordinating this, contact us at **support@amoghsa.com** and we will advise you on the steps.

---

## 4. Apple and Google purchase records

TrackMoola offers **one-time purchases** processed entirely by Apple (App Store / StoreKit) and Google (Google Play Billing). We receive only an entitlement signal to unlock features — we do not store your payment details.

Purchase transaction records are held by Apple and Google, not by us. To manage or request deletion of those records:

- **Apple:** Visit [privacy.apple.com](https://privacy.apple.com) or contact Apple Support.
- **Google:** Visit [myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy](https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy) or contact Google Play Support.

---

## 5. Requesting deletion via email

Although all meaningful deletion can be done by you without contacting us (see Sections 1–3 above), you may also submit a deletion request by email:

**support@amoghsa.com**

Please include:

- The platform (iOS or Android) and the approximate account/device identifier if available.
- A description of what data you believe we may hold (for example, a support ticket you sent us previously).

We will respond within **30 days**. Note that because your financial data is stored only on your device and in your own cloud accounts, there is typically nothing for us to delete on our side beyond any support correspondence we have received from you.
